WHY KLYO

The CRM should fit the work.
Not the other way around.

Make It Yours shapes your workspace. The Quote Creator shapes your proposals. Klyo connects both to your sales and delivery workflow. Here is what that means in a normal working day.

01 / MAKE IT YOURS

Start with what you actually need.

Open Make It Yours in Settings, move or tilt your widgets and put your targets beside the next action. Your layout belongs to your account, so colleagues keep theirs.

A complete offer, without showing every price.

Design your A4 template once. Quote a full computer, list every component, disclose the CPU/GPU sub-group price and apply separate discounts without charging twice. Show internal margins only when you choose.

Is Klyo right for your team?

A good fit when…

You want a flexible sales CRM, detailed product proposals, a dashboard each user can arrange and operational delivery records in the same workspace.

Know the scope

Klyo is not an accounting or KSeF filing system. Provider integrations require configuration, currency reports stay separate, and automated email follow-ups are not included. Compare those requirements against your own workflow.
