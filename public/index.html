A FEW PRACTICAL DETAILS

Before your
first deal.

Will my changes affect the team?

No. Colours, layout, menu visibility and contact columns are saved for your account. Teammates choose their own setup.

Can I choose my own background and accent?

Yes. Start from six presets or create a custom palette. You can set background, panels, accent, navigation and text, with automatic contrast or a manually chosen text colour.

Can I move things around?

Yes. Choose left or right navigation, reorder or hide menu items and contact columns, and arrange the widgets in your overview.

Does Klyo send emails automatically?

No. Templates and the assistant prepare drafts. You review the message and click Send yourself.

How does the trial work?

New workspaces have a seven-day trial. Review available plans and limits inside your workspace before upgrading.
