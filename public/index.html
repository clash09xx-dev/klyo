GETTING STARTED

A few practical details.

Can I bring my existing contacts?

Yes. Import contacts from CSV or Excel files, then organise them with companies, owners and tags.

Can I use my own sales stages?

Yes. Add, rename and reorder the stages in your pipeline, and move deals between them.

Does Klyo send follow-ups automatically?

No. You set the next task and choose when to send a message. Templates and drafts help you prepare the message.

How do I send email?

Connect your email sending account in Settings, then review a message and click Send.

How does the trial work?

New workspaces start with a seven-day trial. Review plans and their limits inside your workspace before upgrading.
