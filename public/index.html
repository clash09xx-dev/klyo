A FEW DETAILS

Before you get started.

Can I bring my existing contacts?

Yes. Import contacts from CSV or Excel files, then organise them with companies, owners and tags.

Can I use my own sales stages?

Yes. Add, rename and reorder the stages in your pipeline. Move deals between stages as your work progresses.

Does Klyo send follow-ups automatically?

No. You choose the next step and decide when a message is sent. Saved templates help you write without scheduling automated outreach.

Can I send quotes and email?

Yes. Create quotes with products, quantities and discounts. Connect your email sending account in Settings before sending messages.

How does the trial work?

New workspaces start with a seven-day trial. You can review the available plans and their limits in your workspace before upgrading.
