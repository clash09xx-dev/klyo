CUSTOMIZE THE WAY YOU WORK

Go beyond
a different colour.

Your CRM should reflect the information you track and the way you sell. Klyo lets you shape the work surface and the records underneath it.

01 / RECORDS

Your own client & product fields

Add text, longer notes, numbers, dates, choice lists and checkboxes. Make a field required when the information matters. Fields belong to the Team or Personal space where you create them.

02 / PROPOSALS

Templates with your name on them

Start from a blank A4 page. Add text, images, shapes and automatic client, date and price fields. Drag, resize and position every element, arrange layers and add pages. Save your own template, import CSV or Excel price lists, and apply line or whole-quote discounts.

03 / SALES

A pipeline for each process

Create separate pipelines for products, services or another sales process. Name them, explain their purpose and set their stages. Connect parent companies and subsidiaries.

04 / WORKSPACE

Make It Yours · layout & focus

Start with six themes or build your palette. Move navigation, choose density, reorder or hide menu items and contact columns, and freely arrange, resize or tilt dashboard widgets.

ONE ACCOUNT. TWO CLEAR SPACES.

Team when you share.
Personal when it’s yours.

A simple switch keeps shared clients and products separate from your own records. Personal work stays private to you.

TEAM

Work together.

  • Shared clients, companies, products and deals
  • Teammates can edit shared records, subject to their role
  • The owner manages invitations and roles
  • Owner-only team activity logs and performance statistics
PERSONAL

Keep your own space.

  • Only you can view and edit your records
  • Your own fields, proposals and sales pipelines
  • Copy all team products, all clients or selected records
  • Copies stay independent; private edits don’t change Team
Bring over only what you need.

Pick a few products, a set of clients or all business records. Linked companies come with selected clients. Quotes and emails are copied as drafts. Repeating a copy preserves your existing personal edits.
