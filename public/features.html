MAKE IT YOURS

Make It Yours

Make It Yours workspace editor

Choose Classic, Explorer, Notebook or Command for the entire application. Build a personal layout with your own navigation, icons, typography, page panels and shortcuts. Save up to six layouts.

Shortcuts across the workspace

Place shortcuts for a new deal, contact, quote or task next to the information you use. Place global shortcuts in the header, work area or navigation footer.

Readable navigation

Group modules into sections, use a simple list or choose two-column tiles. Adjust the menu width, move it left, right or to the top, rename sections and hide unused modules without deleting data.

Your palette and columns

Pick a theme or set your own background, panels and accent with automatic contrast. Reorder contact columns, change density and choose your starting page.

SALES

Stay on top of the sale

Contacts and companies

Connect people, owners, roles, tags, notes, company details, parent companies, subsidiaries, branches and departments.

Multiple sales pipelines

Create named pipelines with their own purpose and stages. Use a board or table and keep independent processes separate.

Won and Lost workspaces

Open dedicated results views, inspect the underlying deals and recorded loss reasons, and review value, win rate and time to win.

Targets and benchmarks

Set monthly targets for sales, new pipeline value, meetings and win rate. Inspect actual results, weighted forecasts, sources, age bands and pipeline health.

Saved views and bulk changes

Keep useful filters, select records for supported bulk changes and export selected deals or reports.

Import and data hygiene

Bring contacts and product data from spreadsheets with mapping and review. Find and merge duplicate contacts while retaining their history.

QUOTES

Send a proposal built for the customer

Quote Creator · A4 designer

Start from a blank A4 page. Position and resize a logo, text, shapes, automatic dates and client fields, pricing tables and totals. Reuse your own templates.

Nested product bundles

Group a complete computer, then group its CPU and GPU inside it. List all components while choosing which group or component prices to disclose.

Layered discounts

Apply item discounts, sub-group discounts, parent discounts and a whole-quote discount in a defined order with exact cent allocation.

Private margin view

Explicitly show or hide purchase costs, profit, margin and markup while composing. Owner-controlled access; internal costs are excluded from customer PDFs.

PDF preview and email

Generate the actual PDF, preview and download it, or attach the approved file to a message you choose to send.

Price lists and commercial register

Import CSV/XLSX price lists, choose a price list while quoting, track case/offer references and review payment and delivery terms in the register.

Optional email assistant

Describe the message in your language or request another output language. Review the draft and send it yourself; no automatic follow-ups.

DELIVERY

Keep delivery connected to the deal

Projects after a win

Future won deals can create one project automatically. The owner can disable this, and existing projects are reused.

Project planning and settlement

Link tasks, people, planned and actual hours, orders, documents and agreements. Owners review separate-currency revenue, purchasing, labor, expenses and profitability.

Orders and confirmations

Manage customer, supplier and internal orders, serial numbers, delivery dates, acknowledgment, invoice references and payments. Generate a customized confirmation PDF.

Equipment and calibration

Track instruments, serial numbers, condition, calibration dates, reservations, check-out and returns. Conflicting reservations are blocked.

Tasks, Kanban and priorities

Use dated tasks, sub-tasks, milestones, reminders, a Kanban board and an urgency/importance matrix.

Warranty and service

Associate purchased products with customers, warranty periods and review dates. See due service reminders in the CRM.

INTEGRATIONS

Connect the tools you already use

Google and Outlook calendars

Connect your private calendar, choose a calendar, view external events and explicitly copy or publish CRM appointments. Export CRM events as ICS.

Microsoft 365 and RFQs

Import authorized mailboxes, including delegated shared mailboxes, link correspondence and attachments to CRM records and configure future inbound RFQ rules and SLA deadlines.

SharePoint, Drive and Dropbox

With configured provider access, browse the selected CRM folder, upload, download and link files to records. Provider account permissions and file limits apply.

Apollo.io import

Search and enrich contacts using your own authorized API key. Confirm credit use before enrichment and deduplicate imported contacts.

DHL and invoice providers

Fetch DHL tracking with your own approved API key. Other carriers use official tracking portals. Configure Fakturownia separately to review and explicitly issue invoices.

TEAM

Work together, keep personal work private

Team and Personal

Switch between shared team records and your private workspace. Copy selected supported records from Team into independent Personal copies.

Owner and access controls

The team owner manages invitations, roles, audit history and internal margin access. The Platform administration page remains restricted to the platform owner.

SMS two-step verification

Connect Twilio Verify at deployment level, then enroll each account with a verified phone. Password and Google sign-in use the extra code when enabled. One-time recovery codes cover SMS outages.

Custom record fields

Create your own fields for customer, company and product characteristics rather than forcing your process into fixed labels.
